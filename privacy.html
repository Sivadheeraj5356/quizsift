Privacy

Last updated: 18 October 2026.

What QuizSift is

QuizSift is a static website. We have no user accounts, no database, and no server-side application that receives quiz content or quiz responses.

Quiz content

When you build a quiz, it is encoded into the share link itself. Anyone who has the link can read the full quiz content. Do not put private or sensitive information in a quiz.

Quiz responses and lead capture

Answers are scored inside the respondent's browser. If the quiz creator enabled lead capture, the name, email, answers and score are sent directly from the respondent's browser to a form endpoint chosen and owned by the creator (for example their account at a form service). That submission is governed by the creator's policies and the form service's privacy policy. QuizSift never receives, stores, or has access to these submissions.

Hosting and logs

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may keep standard server logs (IP address, user agent) for security and operations; see GitHub's privacy statement for details.

Cookies and tracking

QuizSift sets no cookies and runs no analytics or advertising trackers.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue on the project's public GitHub repository.
