Product recommendation quiz: "which one is right for me?" answered automatically

Stores and SaaS pricing pages lose visitors to one question: "which of these is for me?" A product recommendation quiz answers it in under a minute. The visitor describes their situation, the score maps them to a plan, product tier, or category, and the result page links straight to it.

Design questions that map to your tiers

  • Scale. "How many people will use it?" separates individual from team plans.
  • Priorities. "What matters most - price, simplicity, integrations, support?" maps directly to plan positioning.
  • Usage intensity. Occasional vs business-critical usage is often the real tier boundary.

Make every outcome useful

Each score band should name one recommendation and explain it in a sentence - then link to it. Avoid recommending the expensive option to everyone; a recommendation engine that always says "buy the big one" stops being believed, and the honest match converts better anyway.

Beyond plans

The same mechanic works for "which service package", "which course level", "which template bundle" - anywhere a visitor must self-select among options. If your buyer has to think, a quiz thinks with them.
