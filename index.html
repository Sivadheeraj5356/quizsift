Questions

Asked before, answered here

Is QuizSift free?

Yes. Everything that exists today is free - no account, no card. Paid ideas like hosted dashboards and analytics are still on the whiteboard.

Do I need to code?

No. The builder is point and click: questions, points, outcomes, done.

What can I use a scored quiz for?

Lead qualification, product recommenders, knowledge checks and audience segmentation - anywhere a score tells you who someone is.

Where is my quiz stored?

Inside its own share link. There is no QuizSift account or database, and scoring runs in the taker's browser.

How do I collect leads?

Enable lead capture and takers can leave a name and email before their result. Submissions go straight to a form endpoint you own - QuizSift never sees them.

Does it work on phones?

Yes. The builder and the quizzes run in any modern browser, on phones and desktops.
