How it works

  1. Write questions and points. Each answer option carries points. Start from a lead-qualification, product-recommendation, or website-audit template, or from scratch.
  2. Set outcomes by score. "0-6: early stage", "13-20: book a call this week" - each taker lands on the outcome their score earns, with your call to action.
  3. Share one link. Your quiz is encoded in the link itself, so there is nothing to host and nothing for us to store. Post it anywhere people can click.
  4. Capture the lead (optional). Ask for name and email before the result. Submissions go straight from the taker's browser to your own form endpoint - they never pass through QuizSift.

What QuizSift is - and is not

QuizSift is a free, browser-based scored quiz builder. There is no account system and no database on our side: your quiz is stored inside its share link, scoring happens in the taker's browser, and lead capture goes to a form endpoint you choose and control.

It is not a full marketing platform. There are no analytics dashboards, no conditional branching, and no built-in CRM. If you need those, established paid tools like ScoreApp, Typeform, Interact or Outgrow offer them. If you need a fast, free way to qualify leads with a scored quiz, that is exactly what this is.
