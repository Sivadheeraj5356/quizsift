A free Typeform alternative for scored quizzes

Typeform is excellent at conversational forms, and plenty of people first try to build a quiz there. The friction starts when the quiz needs scoring - points per answer, a total, and a different result page per score band. That logic sits behind paid plans, and response limits apply on lower tiers. Check Typeform's site for current plan details; they change.

If what you actually need is a scored quiz

QuizSift does one job: scored quizzes with outcome pages.

  • Points per answer. Every option carries points; the total decides the outcome.
  • Outcome bands. "0-6", "7-12", "13-20" - each with its own verdict text and optional button link.
  • One share link. The whole quiz lives in the link. No embeds, no hosting, no account.
  • Optional lead capture. Collect name and email before the result; submissions go straight to your own form endpoint, never to us.

Where Typeform still wins

Long forms with many field types, payment collection, complex logic jumps, and a big integration library - Typeform is the broader tool. If your quiz is really a form with a score stapled on, compare both honestly. If the score is the point, a focused tool is simpler and, here, free.

Load the product recommendation template in the builder: four questions route each visitor to the plan that fits them. That is the shape of quiz Typeform users most often build - and it takes minutes here.
