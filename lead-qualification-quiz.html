Lead qualification quiz: score leads before you spend an hour on them

Every founder and freelancer knows the pattern: a "hot lead" books a call, and ten minutes in you learn there is no budget, no timeline, and no decision-maker. A lead qualification quiz moves that discovery before the call - the lead answers a few questions, gets something useful back (their score and a tailored verdict), and you get a ranked pipeline instead of a pile of emails.

The four dimensions worth scoring

  • Budget. "Do you have budget set aside?" is worth more points than any other single answer.
  • Timeline. "This month" beats "someday" by a wide margin - weight it that way.
  • Authority. A founder's "yes" and an intern's "yes" are different leads.
  • Pain. Someone paying for a tool that disappoints them is closer to switching than someone content.

What the taker gets

A quiz only converts if the taker wins too. Each score band should hand them something honest: a low score gets useful resources and no pressure, a middle score gets a way to stay in touch, and a high score gets the fast lane - your booking link right on the result page.

Where to put the link

  • In your social bio and pinned post, as the "are we a fit?" path.
  • In your email signature and proposal follow-ups.
  • In community answers where people ask "do I need X?" - answer, then offer the scorecard.
