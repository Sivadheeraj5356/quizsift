A free ScoreApp alternative for scored lead-qualification quizzes

ScoreApp popularised the "scorecard" quiz: a visitor answers questions, gets a score with a personalised verdict, and the business gets a qualified lead. It is a strong pattern - and it does not have to cost a subscription. QuizSift is a free way to run the same core play.

What ScoreApp gives you

ScoreApp is a full scorecard marketing platform: drag-and-drop building, dynamic result pages, integrations with email and CRM tools, and analytics. It is a paid product with tiered plans - check their site for current pricing, since plans and limits change over time. If you run high-volume funnels and need deep integrations, that kind of platform earns its fee.

Where QuizSift is different

  • Free, with no account. You build in the browser and publish by copying a link. There is nothing to sign up for and no trial to expire.
  • Nothing to host. The quiz is encoded in its share link, so it works from a bio, a post, a DM, or an email without embed code.
  • Your leads stay yours. Optional lead capture posts directly from the taker's browser to a form endpoint you own (Formspree, Tally, Web3Forms - your choice). QuizSift never receives or stores submissions.
  • Instant outcomes. Score bands map each total to a tailored verdict and a call-to-action button - book a call, see a plan, read a guide.

Honest trade-offs

QuizSift is deliberately smaller than a platform like ScoreApp. There are no analytics dashboards, no conditional branching, no A/B testing, and no built-in CRM. The quiz content is readable by anyone who has the link, so treat it as public. If you outgrow it, that is a good problem to have.

Get a quiz live in five minutes

  1. Open the builder and load the lead qualification template.
  2. Rewrite the questions around how you qualify buyers: budget, timeline, authority, pain.
  3. Set two or three outcomes with a booking link on the top band.
  4. Paste your form endpoint if you want emails captured, then share the link.
